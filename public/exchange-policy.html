Exchange Policy

Our commitment to your satisfaction.

At WatchLux, we strive to ensure you are completely satisfied with your purchase. If you receive a defective or incorrect item, we offer an exchange under the following terms:

Eligibility for Exchange

Items must be in their original condition, unused, with all original packaging and accessories intact. Exchange requests must be made within 3 days of delivery.

Exchange Process

Contact us via WhatsApp with your order ID and details about the issue. We will guide you through the exchange process. Customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective.

Defective Items

If you receive a defective product, we will replace it at no additional cost. Please notify us within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence of the defect.

Non-Exchangeable Items

Items that have been used, damaged due to misuse, or without original packaging are not eligible for exchange.

For any exchange inquiries, please contact us on WhatsApp.
